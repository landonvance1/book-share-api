Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-30

Data We Collect

We collect the minimum information needed to operate the service:

  • Account information: name and email address
  • App activity: books in your library, lending transactions, and chat messages exchanged with other members
  • Operational logs: basic request logs used solely for maintaining and debugging the service, not for tracking or profiling

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to operate Community Bookshare — matching borrowers with lenders, enabling in-app messaging, and managing your library. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

Data Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text. We use industry-standard hashing (PBKDF2) to protect your credentials.

Account Deletion

Users may permanently delete their account at any time via the app settings. Upon deletion:

  • All personal information (name, email, password) is immediately and permanently removed from our systems.
  • Active login sessions and refresh tokens are revoked.
  • Personal notifications are deleted.

Retained Anonymized Data

To preserve the continuity of experience for other users who were party to a book-lending transaction or chat conversation with the deleted user, we retain anonymized transaction records and chat thread structure. Specifically:

  • Share (lending transaction) records are kept but display the deleted user as "[Deleted User]".
  • Chat messages sent by the deleted user have their content replaced with "[deleted]"; the message structure is retained so conversation history for other participants remains coherent.
  • Community records created by the deleted user remain intact for remaining members.

This retention is limited to data that benefits other parties and contains no personally identifiable information. It is consistent with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and applicable privacy regulations' carve-outs for operational necessity.
